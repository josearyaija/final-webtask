CASE STUDY SPOTLIGHT

Walnut House: A Cotswolds commission.

A comprehensive joinery suite spanning kitchen, library, and dining room for an 18th-century stone barn conversion.

Natural stone meets
dark American walnut.
THE ARCHITECTURAL BRIEF

Balancing heritage stonework with modern cabinetry.

The client required cabinetry that respected the rough Cotswolds lime mortar walls without retreating into pastiche country-cottage tropes. We selected rich, dark American walnut with subtle chocolate undertones to ground the high double-height barn spaces.

Every drawer box was hand-cut with half-blind dovetails from quarter-sawn English sycamore, offering a dramatic pale contrast whenever a drawer is opened. Finished in matte organic hardwax oil for maximum durability against daily family life.
