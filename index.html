Where tradition meets modern design.

Forma is an independent woodworking studio creating custom furniture and considered interiors. We bring time-honoured joinery together with a clear, contemporary architectural point of view.

From the first concept sketch to the final on-site fitting, we believe the best work comes from listening closely, choosing sustainably sourced timbers with care, and sweating every millimeter.
