STEP-BY-STEP WORKSHOP JOURNEY

Our 6-stage methodology.

How an initial concept develops into an heirloom piece built for generations of daily use.

01
STAGE 01 / CONVERSATION

Consultation & Spatial Brief

We begin with a conversation—either at our Clerkenwell design studio, your home, or via video call. We study your room's natural lighting, lifestyle routines, functional storage needs, and aesthetic reference points. We discuss preliminary dimensions and timber preferences.

Stage Deliverables

  • Initial spatial brief & functional scope
  • Ballpark investment estimate & timeline schedule
  • Curated moodboard & joinery direction
02
STAGE 02 / DESIGN

Concept Design & 3D CAD Planning

Our studio translates the brief into millimeter-precise CAD drawings and photorealistic 3D concept renders. We model how doors pivot, how drawers open, and how joinery interfaces with architectural walls, skirting boards, and electrical outlets.

Stage Deliverables

  • Dimensioned orthographic CAD elevations
  • 3D spatial perspective visualizations
  • Physical timber & finish sample swatches posted to you
03
STAGE 03 / SELECTION

Timber Sourcing & Flitch Matching

Once drawings and finishes are formally approved, we visit our sustainable timber merchant partners in person. We hand-select consecutive boards cut from the same tree trunk (known as a flitch) to guarantee harmonious grain continuity, color tone, and density.

Stage Deliverables

  • Photos of selected raw timber flitches
  • Kiln-dry moisture verification certificate (8–10%)
  • FSC/PEFC sustainable chain-of-custody confirmation
04
STAGE 04 / BENCHWORK

Workshop Handcrafting & Joinery

At our workshop in the Cotswolds, our master makers begin milling, dimensioning, and cutting joinery. We employ true mortise-and-tenons, hand-cut dovetails for drawers, and slotted expansion brackets that allow the solid wood to breathe with changing humidity.

Stage Deliverables

  • Mid-build workshop progress photos & video updates
  • Dry-fit assembly check and joint tolerances
  • Optional workshop visit to see your piece on the bench
05
STAGE 05 / FINISHING

Hand-Sanding & Multi-Coat Oil Finishing

The piece is progressively hand-sanded from 120 through 320 grit until surfaces feel silky to the touch. We hand-rub multiple layers of European hardwax oil and natural beeswax, allowing each coat to cure in our dust-free finishing room before final buffing.

Stage Deliverables

  • Inspection under multi-directional studio lighting
  • Full mechanical hardware testing & calibration
  • Custom Forma care guide & maintenance oil pot
06
STAGE 06 / COMPLETION

White-Glove Delivery & On-Site Installation

We believe the makers should be present for the installation. Our team delivers your piece using blanket-wrapped white-glove transport, scribes built-in cabinetry against uneven heritage walls, secures anchors, and performs final on-site leveling and buffing.

Stage Deliverables

  • Direct delivery by our own workshop personnel
  • Precision on-site scribing and installation
  • Lifetime structural joinery guarantee
ENGINEERED FOR LONGEVITY

Why bespoke joinery endures.

A look at the structural physics behind our woodwork.

Seasonal Expansion Relief

Wood is hygroscopic; it absorbs and releases atmospheric moisture. We design slotted brass tabletop clips and breadboard ends that let the wood move horizontally without warping or splitting.

End-Grain Sealing

The porous end-grain of timber absorbs moisture 10 times faster than face-grain. We saturate all hidden end-grain surfaces with deep-penetrating oil to prevent uneven drying.

Spot-Repairable Surfaces

Unlike plastic polyurethane coats that require complete stripping when scratched, our natural hardwax finish can be spot-repaired with a dab of oil on a cloth in under two minutes.
