01 / CULINARY SPACES

Handcrafted Kitchen Cabinetry

We build kitchens meant to be lived in, cooked in, and gathered around. Every carcass is crafted from solid multi-layer hardwood ply with solid wood face frames, hand-dovetailed drawer boxes, and continuous grain matching across all drawer fronts.

  • Solid oak or walnut dovetailed drawers
  • Concealed soft-close Blumotion hardware
  • Integrated cutlery organizers & pantries
  • Water-resistant micro-porous hardwax seals

Freestanding Custom Furniture

Credenzas, media consoles, accent cabinets, and low coffee tables designed with sculptural restraint. We prioritize proportional harmony, tactile edge chamfers, and through-tenon joint details that celebrate genuine craftsmanship.

COMMISSIONING ADVISORY

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about commissioning custom woodwork with Forma.

What is your typical lead time?

Freestanding furniture commissions typically take 6 to 10 weeks from deposit and drawing approval. Complete kitchen cabinetry or extensive architectural millwork averages 10 to 14 weeks depending on scale.

Can you work with my interior designer?

Yes. A significant portion of our commissions comes through partnerships with architects and interior design practices. We provide detailed CAD drawings, timber finish samples, and site coordination.

Do you deliver and install on-site?

Always. Our master makers personally handle or supervise the delivery and installation of all fitted joinery and statement pieces to ensure flawless scribing and levelling.
